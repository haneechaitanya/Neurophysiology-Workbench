# Generative-AI assistance disclosure

Neurophysiology Workbench, including its initial ERP Workbench application, is
a human-directed, AI-assisted scientific software project.

The project and its ERP workflow were conceived and scientifically specified by **H. C. Challa**
(ORCID: https://orcid.org/0009-0009-3546-0027). H. C. Challa defined the intended
EEG/ERP workflows, processing requirements, user interactions, methodological
constraints, testing scenarios, and release priorities, and evaluated the
application through iterative use and revision.

Generative-AI systems, including OpenAI's ChatGPT and Google's Gemini, were used
extensively to draft, explain, revise, debug, and test source code and
documentation in response to detailed human instructions and feedback.
Accordingly, significant portions of the implementation were produced through
AI-assisted programming rather than being typed line-by-line by the project
author.

The AI systems did not independently originate the project's scientific goals,
do not hold authorship or copyright, and are not responsible for the software.
The project maintainer selects and integrates the material included in releases
and remains responsible for testing, scientific review, dependency compliance,
security review, and communicating known limitations. Such review is ongoing,
particularly for the ICA workflow, which remains explicitly marked BETA.

Mention of an AI provider or product documents the development process. It does
not imply sponsorship, certification, or endorsement of ERP Workbench by that
provider.

This disclosure may be updated when the development process or principal tools
change materially.
