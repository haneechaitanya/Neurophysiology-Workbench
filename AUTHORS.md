## Future contributors

Human contributors should be credited here when their accepted contribution is
substantial enough to warrant project-level attribution. Individual code
contributions remain traceable through the Git history.

Third-party libraries and their developers are acknowledged separately in
`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`; using a dependency does not make its developers
authors of ERP Workbench or imply that they endorse this project.
